**App 名称**

```

**副标题**

```

**宣传文本**
```
个人辅导老师的口袋账本：10 秒记一笔课时，课包自动扣减，月底一键生成账单分享家长。数据存本机，完全离线，无需登录。
```

**描述**
```
课酬记是面向个人学科辅导老师的课时费记录工具。下课记一笔，课包自动扣、待收自动挂，月底跟家长对账有依据——不做机构教务系统，只做你口袋里的课酬账本。

【10 秒记课时】
选学生、确认时长与单价，保存即入账。默认单价从学生档案带出，并保存当次价格快照，事后不会算错。

【三种计费，各走各的账】
• 先付课包：购课入库，上课自动扣节
• 课后结算：课时挂待收，月底汇总出账
• 按次现结：当场可标记已收款

【账单对账，说得清楚】
按学生汇总待出账课时，一键生成账单；标记待发送 / 已发送 / 已收款，分享 CSV 给家长核对。

【课包可追溯】
购课、扣课、作废返还流水按时间排列，家长问「还剩几节」打开即答。

【统计与备份】
按月查看课时、时长与已收；设置页一键导出全部数据 CSV。建议换机前定期备份。

【隐私与离线】
无需注册登录，数据仅保存在本机，核心功能完全离线可用。当前版本不含广告、不含内购。

使用条款 (EULA)：https://github.com/skrhhh/TutorLedger_setting/blob/main/Terms-of-Service.md
隐私政策：https://github.com/skrhhh/TutorLedger_setting/blob/main/Privacy-Policy.md
```

## English (en)

**App Name**
```
TutorLedger

**Subtitle**
```
Lesson pay made simple
```

**Promotional Text**
```
Your pocket ledger for tutoring pay: log a lesson in 10 seconds, auto-deduct packages, generate bills to share with parents. Fully offline—data stays on your device.
```

**Description**
```
TutorLedger helps individual subject tutors record lessons, track packages, and settle pay clearly. Log after class, auto-deduct prepaid hours or park postpaid amounts, then generate a bill parents can verify—no heavy school admin system, just a pocket ledger for tutoring pay.

LOG A LESSON IN SECONDS
Pick a student, confirm duration and rate, and save. Default rates come from the student profile, with a per-lesson price snapshot so amounts stay accurate later.

THREE BILLING MODES
• Prepaid package — purchase sessions, deduct on each lesson
• Pay after class — lessons stay pending until you issue a bill
• Pay per session — mark paid on the spot when needed

BILLS YOU CAN EXPLAIN
Group pending lessons by student, generate a bill, mark draft / sent / paid, and share a CSV for parent reconciliation.

PACKAGE HISTORY
Purchases, deductions, and void refunds are listed in order—so “how many sessions left?” is one tap away.

STATS & BACKUP
See monthly lessons, hours, and received pay. Export a full CSV from Settings. Back up before you switch phones.

PRIVATE & OFFLINE
No sign-in. Data stays on your device. Core features work fully offline. No ads and no in-app purchases in this version.
